# Accounting by Amir Gill: topic queue, Dec 2026 to May 2027

Every topic comes from the official Cambridge syllabuses for the June 2027 exams:
- O Level Accounting 7707 (2027–2029 syllabus)
- IGCSE 0452, which has the same content
- AS & A Level Accounting 9706 (2026–2028 syllabus)

Topics alternate between O Level and A Level, so both groups of students see posts every week. A topic shared by both levels is tagged for both.

## How to load it
1. **Start the first timeline.** On the Timeline tab, start it by hand, or send this in Discord:
   `topic: The accounting equation and double entry basics | 7 | O Level 7707 & IGCSE 0452`
2. **Fill the queue.** Paste the block below into **Up next** and press **Save queue**. Each timeline then starts the next topic the day after it ends, with no further action.
3. **Set the ad days.** Set **Ad every Nth day** to **7** for December and January, so only the 7-day topics carry an advert. Change it to **5** on 1 February, when the paid ads start.

## Notes
- **Late start:** if posting starts after mid-December, delete one or two A2 lines from the January block. That keeps the exam-technique posts in early May.
- **Check the exam dates:** confirm the June 2027 timetable once Cambridge publishes it. If a paper falls before 9 May, move rows 30–31 earlier.
- **Mid-run changes:** add a topic from Discord with `queue: Topic | days | level`, or edit the **Up next** box at any time. The order in the box is the order things run.
- **After a post:** a matching YouTube video from Amir's channel is linked automatically, whenever one fits that day's topic.
